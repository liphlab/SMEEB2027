Venue and logistics

How to get to the conference

How to get to Venice

By plane. The closest airport is Venice Marco Polo (VCE), about 13 km from the city. From the airport you can reach Piazzale Roma — the road terminus of Venice, where all buses and taxis stop — by ATVO express bus or by ACTV bus line 5, in roughly 20–25 minutes. Alternatively, Alilaguna water buses connect the airport directly to several stops in Venice; this is slower but more scenic.

A second option is Treviso (TSF), about 40 km away and mostly served by low-cost carriers, connected to Piazzale Roma by ATVO bus in about 70 minutes.

By train. Venice has two main stations. Trains stop at Venezia Mestre, on the mainland, and most continue to Venezia Santa Lucia, which is in the historical centre and is the one you want. Both are served by Trenitalia and Italo, with frequent high-speed connections to Milan, Bologna, Florence and Rome.

Getting around. There are no cars in Venice: from Piazzale Roma or Santa Lucia you move on foot or by vaporetto (water bus), operated by ACTV. If you are staying several days, a multi-day travel pass is usually cheaper than single tickets.
